# 🔎 Smart AI Lost & Found

## Problem

People often lose personal belongings in crowded places like railway stations, colleges, airports, malls, and public transport. Existing lost-and-found systems rely heavily on manual searching through posts and descriptions, making it difficult to connect the rightful owner with a found item.

## Solution

Smart AI Lost & Found is an **AI-powered agent** that automatically identifies potential matches between lost and found items using images and AI reasoning.

## How It Works

```text
Lost Item Image
       ↓
CLIP analyzes the image
       ↓
Finds visually similar found items
       ↓
Gemini compares the best matches
       ↓
AI analyzes color, shape, brand,
marks, patterns, and accessories
       ↓
Potential Match
       ↓
Owner Verification
       ↓
Recovery / Connection
